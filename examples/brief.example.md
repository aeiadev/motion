# Example brief

Use: a quiet website hero behind a short headline.
Output: one self-contained HTML file with inline canvas JavaScript and no network.
System: flow-field; 420 seeded threads follow a smooth direction field.
Palette: --bg #f3ecdf, --ink #16130f, --accent #b5452b.
Loop: 12 seconds, seamless, with slow motion.
Viewport: 1600 x 900; also check a narrow 390 x 700 viewport.
Limit: leave the central headline region quiet and readable.
First steer: a material reference of fine ink on warm paper.

Render seeds 1 to 12 on one sheet and choose by eye. Record the seed here after
selection. Keep it fixed while steering one change per round. Deliver the HTML,
a poster at phase 0, and an MP4 at 30 fps if export tools are installed.
